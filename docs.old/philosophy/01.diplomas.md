# Diplomas

[//]: # (@formatter:off)
/// admonition | Robin, you don't have a diploma.
    type: quote
///
[//]: # (@formatter:on)

I hear this sentence each time I talk with a recruter. I understand the concern, diplomas are a kind of guarantee. Having
one demonstrate, at least partially, that you have the skills and knowledge.

I could say that diplomas are not that important, or any other shenanigans I often hear from the other people without
diplomas. But, I don't think like this. Instead, let me ask you two questions:

[//]: # (@formatter:off)
/// admonition | What does a diploma represent to you?
    type: question
///
[//]: # (@formatter:on)

Surprisingly, not many people asked this question. Although it's a very interesting one. Being the first concerned about
it, here's what I think.

A diploma is way more than a piece of paper, it confirms that you have:

- Skills
- Knowledge
- A minimum amount of experience (especially for the work-study ones)

That is not all. To earn a diploma, you need:

- To be dedicated
- To be focused
- To be autonomous in your learnings
- To be constant in your efforts
- Often to work with others
- Sometimes, to be able to navigate on the administration
- And many more...

[//]: # (@formatter:off)
/// admonition | To sumarize
    type: abstract
Saying "I have this diploma" is a great shortcut to say all above in one sentence.
///
[//]: # (@formatter:on)

In the end, I think that's one of the reasons why diplomas became so important when it comes to hiring people. It's
completely legit to want someone we can rely on: diplomas is one way to prove it.

So, here's the second question :

[//]: # (@formatter:off)
/// admonition | Is it possible to have all these qualities without graduating a diploma?
    type: question
///
[//]: # (@formatter:on)

The answer is yes.

## Pros and cons

Not following the "royal" path of the diplomas comes with benefits and inconvenient. In my personal experience, I listed
the following ones. Spoiler: I happen to find a lot more pros than cons.

### Cons

* We can lack of standard theorical knowledge sometimes
* We lack of processes knowledge and experience
* Learning can be slower due to it parallelism with a job or life period. Being constant requires a lot of discipline
  and/or passion

[//]: # (@formatter:off)
/// admonition | Note
    type: note
Most of the above cons can be fixed with a little professional experience, if we are curious and open-minded to improve the way we 
work. There is hope for the diploma-less people.
///
[//]: # (@formatter:on)

### Pros

* We are fully autonomous in our learning and have the habits to learn new things by ourselves
* We are resourceful: when nobody is there to find a solution for you, you have to take care of all the issues yourself
* Achieving a great level without the pressure of a diploma requires a great level of passion for the domain
